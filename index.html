Region Skåne

Sweden · Health care (breast cancer screening)
Finished
AI risk-scores every screening mammogram, so low-risk exams are read by one radiologist and high-risk exams by two. In the MASAI trial of about 106,000 women, it found 29% more cancers, cut reading workload by 44% and did not increase false positives.
Cost
Not reported (trial funded by Cancerfonden, Lund University/ALF, RCC Samverkan and MAS Cancer)
Duration
Apr 2021 to Dec 2025 (enrolment to Dec 2022, final results Jan 2026)
Delivery
Research-led by Lund University and Skåne University Hospital, using a commercial AI product
AI providers
ScreenPoint Medical (Transpara)
